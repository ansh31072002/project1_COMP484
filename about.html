About the Show

The Big Bang Theory is an American sitcom created by Chuck Lorre and Bill Prady. It aired on CBS from September 2007 to May 2019 and became one of the most-watched comedy series in television history. The show follows a group of Caltech physicists and their friends—and later partners—as they navigate work, relationships, and pop culture, all with a heavy dose of science, sci-fi, and comic-book references.

Fun Facts

  • Real physicists and scientists, including Neil deGrasse Tyson and Stephen Hawking, made cameo appearances on the show.
  • The whiteboard equations in Sheldon and Leonard’s apartment were often real physics and math, vetted by UCLA professor David Saltzberg.
  • “Bazinga!” was ad-libbed by Jim Parsons and became Sheldon’s catchphrase and the title of a later episode.
  • The show won multiple Emmys, including four Lead Actor in a Comedy Series wins for Jim Parsons as Sheldon.
  • Kaley Cuoco (Penny) and Johnny Galecki (Leonard) dated in real life during the early seasons.
  • The apartment set number 4A was a nod to the fourth dimension and other sci-fi tropes.
